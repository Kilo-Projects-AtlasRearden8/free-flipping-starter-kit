What's in the kit

  • Listing templates — Copy-and-fill eBay and Amazon listings in plain English. Honest condition notes build buyer trust.
  • Profit calculators — Test a price before you list. Everything runs in your browser; nothing leaves your computer.
  • Sourcing log — Track every item you buy and sell, on paper or in a spreadsheet.
  • Pricing tips — Price from sold items, know your fees, set a floor price.

How to use

  1. Open a template, copy the text, and fill in your item's details.
  2. Open a calculator in any browser and run the numbers before you list.
  3. Log each sale in the sourcing log so you know what's actually profitable.
  4. Print anything: all pages are designed for clean paper output.
