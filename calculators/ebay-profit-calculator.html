How the math works

  • eBay fee: sale price × fee percentage.
  • Your costs: what you paid + supplies + shipping you pay yourself.
  • Net profit: sale price − eBay fee − your costs.
  • Profit margin: profit ÷ sale price.
  • ROI: profit ÷ what you spent.

This is an estimate only. eBay fees change over time and vary by category. Always check eBay's current fee schedule before you list.
