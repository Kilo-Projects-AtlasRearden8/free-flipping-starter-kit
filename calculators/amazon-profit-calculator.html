How the math works

  • Referral fee: sale price × fee percentage.
  • Total cost: what you paid + supplies + shipping you pay.
  • Net profit: sale price − referral fee − total cost.
  • Margin: profit ÷ sale price.
  • ROI: profit ÷ what you spent.
